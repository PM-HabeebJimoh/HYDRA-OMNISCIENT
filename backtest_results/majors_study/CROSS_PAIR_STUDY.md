# Effect of the Agba Metta Model on ALL Other Currency Pairs — Full Research

**Question:** when XAUUSD + EURUSD + AUDUSD all move the same way (the Agba Metta 3-alignment),
what happens to every other currency pair — and would the model have worked on them?

## Universe tested

| Group | Pairs | Investing.com IDs |
|---|---|---|
| Core (signal-defining) | XAUUSD, EURUSD, AUDUSD | 68, 1, 5 |
| USD-quoted majors | GBPUSD, NZDUSD | 2, 8 |
| USD-base majors | USDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD | 3, 4, 7 |
| **Non-USD crosses** | **EURGBP, EURJPY, GBPJPY** | **6, 11, 9** |

**Sample:** 194 sessions with all five extra USD majors (2023-01-02 → 2026-04-01), of which
**91 are signal days** (43 all-down, 48 all-up). Crosses: 65 sessions / 32 signal days (2026 Q1).
All real daily spot open/close. No synthetic data.

Convention: for XXXUSD pairs a *fall* = USD stronger; for USDXXX pairs a *rise* = USD stronger.

Confirmation breadth on the 91 signal days:

**93.5 % of signal days have at least 3 of 5 other majors confirming.** The 3-alignment is not
telling you something about gold, the euro and the Aussie — it is telling you the dollar moved.

## B. THE DECISIVE TEST — non-USD crosses show NOTHING

If the signal were a genuine market regime, it should show up in pairs that contain no dollar.
It does not:

| Cross | n | Up-rate on signal days | mean | \|t\| | Verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| EURGBP | 32 | 50.0 % | +0.020 % | 0.43 | **noise** |
| EURJPY | 32 | 46.9 % | +0.008 % | 0.09 | **noise** |
| GBPJPY | 32 | 53.1 % | +0.021 % | 0.25 | **noise** |

Exactly 50.0 %, 46.9 %, 53.1 % — coin flips, all |t| < 0.5. **Remove the dollar from the pair and
the signal disappears completely.** This is conclusive: Agba Metta detects USD direction and
nothing else.

## D. Substitution test — swapping even ONE leg breaks the 100 %

Signal always stays gold+EUR+AUD; only the **traded** legs change:

| Traded basket | Day WR | mean/day | worst day |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **gold + eur + aud (ORIGINAL)** | **100.0 %** | +0.8391 % | +0.135 % |
| gold + eur + GBP | 100.0 % | +0.7798 % | +0.010 % |
| gold + eur + NZD | 98.9 % | +0.8119 % | −0.062 % |
| gold + GBP + NZD | 98.9 % | +0.8106 % | −0.008 % |
| gold + eur + JPY | 94.5 % | +0.7162 % | −0.183 % |
| eur + aud + GBP (no gold) | 98.9 % | +0.5629 % | −0.009 % |
| GBP + NZD + CHF (no core) | 93.4 % | +0.5147 % | −0.301 % |

Only the exact basket that *defines* the signal achieves 100 % and a positive worst day. Every
substitution introduces losing days. The perfection is a property of the self-reference, not of
the market.

possibility that the earlier finding was an artifact of the three chosen assets.

## Summary of all findings

1. **The 3-alignment is a US-dollar signal.** All five other USD majors confirm it (corr +0.53 to
   +0.83); 93.5 % of signal days have ≥3 of 5 confirming.
2. **Non-USD crosses show pure noise** — EURGBP 50.0 %, EURJPY 46.9 %, GBPJPY 53.1 %, all |t| < 0.5.
   Take the dollar out and the effect vanishes entirely. This is the definitive proof.
3. **The dollar move is real; the 100 % win rate is not.** Other pairs hit by the identical move
   score 64.8 %–94.5 %. USDJPY at 64.8 % is the clearest measure of the true hit rate.
4. **Swapping even one leg breaks the 100 %** — only the self-referential basket is perfect.
5. **Adding pairs degrades everything** — dilution plus correlation, and the first losing day.
6. **Gold is the engine and the risk** — 2.6× FX volatility, and the only asset the 1 % stop reaches.
7. **Lagged one session, nothing predicts anything** — 8 instruments, all |t| < 2.

**Bottom line for trading:** the universe is not where the edge is. If you keep this structure,
gold alone outperforms the basket on signal days and the stop is sized for gold, so the two FX
legs contribute dilution and almost no protection. The tradeable content of the signal is a
same-day dollar move you cannot observe until the day is already over.
